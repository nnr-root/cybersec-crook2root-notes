# 🍎 macOS

> [!abstract] Sub-index of [[OS Internals]]
> A strict zero-to-mastery curriculum covering XNU, Unix operations, APFS, process supervision, identity, networking, platform defenses, executable loading, and forensic reconstruction.

## 🗺️ Zero-to-Mastery Learning Path

1. [[macOS Darwin and XNU Kernel|macOS Darwin & XNU Kernel]] — understand Mach tasks, threads, virtual memory, ports, BSD, drivers, zones, and Apple Silicon trust.
2. [[macOS CLI and Unix Backend|macOS CLI & Unix Backend]] — develop safe Zsh fluency and operate native system, storage, identity, process, and configuration interfaces.
3. [[macOS APFS and File System|macOS APFS & File System]] — master containers, volume groups, clones, snapshots, encryption, firmlinks, metadata, and application bundles.
4. [[macOS Processes and Daemons|macOS Processes & Daemons]] — trace `launchd` domains, job lifecycles, agents, daemons, XPC, and privileged helpers.
5. [[macOS Identity Keychain & Credentials]] — distinguish Unix identity, Authorization Services, Keychain access, SecureToken, FileVault identities, and enterprise credentials.
6. [[macOS Networking Internals]] — follow traffic through scoped DNS, sockets, routes, `pf`, Application Firewall, Network Extension, and tunnel interfaces.
7. [[macOS Security Mechanisms]] — compose Gatekeeper, notarization, XProtect, AMFI, Hardened Runtime, SIP, TCC, Seatbelt, FileVault, and Endpoint Security.
8. [[macOS Binaries and Logging|macOS Binaries & Runtime Loading]] — dissect Mach-O, universal slices, load commands, `dyld`, shared caches, signatures, and entitlements.
9. [[macOS Observability Incident Response & Forensics]] — collect volatile state, Unified Logs, FSEvents, quarantine, TCC, launch items, snapshots, and corroborated timelines.

## Curriculum Outcomes

- **Crook:** navigate macOS safely and recognize its major architectural boundaries.
- **Operator:** diagnose system behavior with native commands, expected output, and repeatable authorized labs.
- **Root:** reason across kernel, storage, identity, network, execution, trust, and evidence layers without flattening macOS into generic Unix assumptions.
